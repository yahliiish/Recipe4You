Healthy Twix Bar

⏱️
Total Time 30 mins
🍽️
Yield
6 servings

This is perfect for satisfying those chocolate craving, healthy and nourishing but SO good and a perfect sweet treat.

Ingredients

Shortbread layer

Caramel layer

Chocolate layer

Instructions

  1. Step 01

    Mix all the shortbread ingredients and keep in the fridge until needed

  2. Step 02

    Mix all the caramel layer and put it on the shortbread

  3. Step 03

    Melt the chocolate with oil and drizzle on top

  4. Step 04

    Sprinkle a bit sea salt and put in the freezer until ready to eat
