Yogurt bark

⏱️
Total Time 5:10 hours
🍽️
Yield
6 servings

A high protein snack to make ahead of time and take anytime for a quick satisfying snack

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Step 01

    Mix the yogurt with the sweetener of choice and spread it on a flat surface lined with parchment paper.

  2. Step 02

    Add on top your fruits of choice and optional drizzle of peanut butter.

  3. Step 03

    Keep in the freezer for minimum 5 hours.

  4. Step 04

    When frozen, break into 6 or desired amount of pieces.
