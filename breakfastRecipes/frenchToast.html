French Toast

⏱️
Total Time 25 mins
🍽️
Yield
4 servings

As an older sister i can say that this is the easiest and most fun breakfast to spoil your younger siblings on a saturday morning, a birthday, or just on a good mood morning!

Ingredients

Cinnamon sugar

Instructions

  1. Step 01

    Mix the batter ingredients in a bowl

  2. Step 02

    Take the hallah slices and dip it in the batter, let soak for about 15 seconds or when it feels soaked enough

  3. Step 03

    Turn on stofe on medium heat, and oil the pan with butter/ oil.

  4. Step 04

    Let the soaked slices brown up on one side, then you know it's time to flip it to brown the other side

  5. Step 05

    When taken off the pan, place in the cinnamon sugar mess and serve with love
